# scholium

Scholium helps you work across a scholarly manuscript written in Word: find passages and notes, compare arguments, review terminology, save chapter summaries, and investigate the consequences of revisions.

**Start with the [academic user guide](docs/USER_GUIDE.md).** It explains what you can ask and how to use the answers in your writing.

Examples:

> Does my conclusion deliver what the introduction promises?

> Compare these chapters’ uses of belonging and identity. Show the passages that support your reading.

> I revised this argument. Which other passages might need reconsideration?

You continue editing in Word. Codex reads relevant evidence through scholium and offers interpretations with references. Your originals are not edited automatically. Summaries and review decisions can be saved, and outdated readings are identified after chapter changes.
